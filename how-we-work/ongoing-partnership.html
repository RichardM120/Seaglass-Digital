Ongoing partnership

Best for: the challenge is bigger than one fix, or keeps changing shape.

A full discovery of where AI and digital can genuinely help your business, delivery of the fixes that come out of it, and continuous support while they bed in — with your team trained to run everything themselves, not kept dependent on us.

What we do

  • Run a proper discovery across the business, not just one team or one process, to find where AI and digital tools would genuinely save time versus where they'd just add a new thing to ignore
  • Agree a prioritised plan and deliver it, one piece at a time, in the order that pays off fastest
  • Stay involved while each piece beds in, adjusting as we learn how your team actually uses it
  • Train your team to run everything day-to-day, then step back to a check-in roughly every six months rather than an ongoing retainer

What we need from you

  • A senior decision-maker involved through the discovery phase, so priorities stick
  • Time from the people who'll ultimately run things, spread across the engagement rather than all at once
  • Openness about what isn't working across the wider business, not just the one visible pain point

What you walk away with

The kind of AI and digital capability a large enterprise takes for granted, sized and paced for an independent business — run entirely by your own team, with us checking in periodically rather than sitting on a permanent retainer.
