Audit & recommendations

Best for: you want a clear outside view before committing to anything.

A structured review of one part of your business, and a short, written set of prioritised recommendations. No build included — just a plan you can act on, with us or without us.

What we do

  • A half-day, on-site (or remote) look at one specific pain point — a rota, a quoting process, a website, whatever's costing you the most time
  • Look at the tools and workflow you're already using, and where they're leaking time or money
  • Write up a short, prioritised set of recommendations, in plain English, within 48 hours
  • No obligation to go further — the plan is yours either way

What we need from you

  • Half a day of your time, or whoever actually runs the process day-to-day
  • Honest access to what isn't working, not just what looks good on the surface
  • A named contact who can answer questions and make small decisions on the day

What you walk away with

A clear, written plan for one part of your business. Some people take it and run with it themselves. Others bring us back in to fix the problem directly, or to go deeper as an ongoing partner. Either way, the audit is useful on its own.
