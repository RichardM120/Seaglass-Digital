Fix one problem

Best for: you already know what's broken and want it solved.

An evaluation of that one specific challenge, the right digital tool or service chosen and set up around how you already work, and your team trained to run it day-to-day. Fixed fee, agreed upfront.

What we do

  • Take a clear enough starting point — usually an audit, but not required — and evaluate the problem in more detail
  • Choose and configure the right tool or service around your existing workflow, not a rip-and-replace of what already works
  • Run hands-on training with whoever runs it day-to-day, in plain English, until they're genuinely comfortable, not just shown once
  • Agree a fixed fee upfront — no ongoing retainer required to keep it running

What we need from you

  • A specific problem, roughly scoped — we'll sharpen it together if it isn't yet
  • Access to the person or team who'll actually run the tool once it's live
  • A willingness to adjust the existing process slightly, rather than just bolting a tool on top of it

What you walk away with

One pain point, fixed, in your team's hands. You own everything we build — no annual lock-in, no dependency on us to keep it running.
