Everything you were wondering but didn't want to ask yet.

Straight answers, no jargon

This is where you can find out more about how SeaGlass Digital works, from pricing to process to what happens after a project ends. If there's something we haven't answered below, just ask.

Ask us directly
Is my business the right fit for this?

There's no fixed size or sector we work with. It depends on the challenges that need solving, not your headcount or turnover. If you're big enough to be drowning in admin but too small for a head office, that's usually the sweet spot. The best way to find out is to talk it through.

What happens after I get in touch?

We start by gathering information and identifying where we believe AI can help the most. From there, we send a short recommendation proposal, then connect to review it together and agree the project before any work begins.

How much does this cost?

During the initial conversation we work out the scale and complexity of the job together, and agree a fixed fee upfront, with no hidden surprises. We keep costs to a minimum by using proven steps and AI tooling wherever we can.

How long does an engagement take?

It varies hugely: some projects are a simple one or two day session, others run six to eight weeks depending on size and complexity. Whatever the scope, we minimise your time and are upfront about what's needed right from the start.

Do we need to meet in person?

We like to connect face to face wherever possible, but we know how busy small business owners are, so we're just as happy over Zoom or on the phone. Every customer is different, so we agree the format that works for you at the start.

Do I need to know anything about AI already?

No background required. There's a lot of noise about AI right now and you don't need to have followed any of it. We explain everything in plain English, and no question is too basic to ask.

What if AI isn't the right answer for my business?

Then we'll tell you plainly. Our job isn't to sell you AI for the sake of it. It's to find the two or three places it could genuinely help, and be honest if the answer is that it can't.

What happens after the project ends?

You own everything we build, and there's no annual lock-in or ongoing contract. After every engagement we check in to make sure everything's working well, and if you'd like ongoing support later, we agree that approach at the time.
