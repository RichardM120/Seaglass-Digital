How we work

Four stages. One fixed fee. Nothing left running that your team can't run themselves.

Every engagement follows the same shape, whether the pain point is a rota, a quoting process, or a website that no longer sounds like you. Small scope, real handover, no dependency on us afterwards.

The four stages

1

Audit

A half-day, on-site look at one specific pain point. You get a short written action plan within 48 hours, no obligation to go further.

→
2

Fit

We choose and configure the right tool around how you already work, not the other way round. No rip-and-replace of what already works.

→
3

Train

Hands-on sessions with your team, in plain English, until whoever runs it day-to-day is genuinely comfortable, not just shown once.

→
4

Own it

Fixed fee, agreed upfront. You own everything we build. No annual lock-in, no ongoing retainer required to keep it running.

That's it. No lengthy discovery phase, no methodology deck, no quarterly review calls. One pain point, fixed, in your team's hands.
