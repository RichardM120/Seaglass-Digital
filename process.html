How we work

Three options, no lock-in. You decide how far to go.

Whether the pain point is a rota, an accounting process, or a website update you cannot remember how to do, every engagement follows the same shape: small scope, real handover, no dependency on us afterwards. Most people start with a review; if it turns up something worth fixing, that rolls straight into a Fix, and if the challenge keeps changing shape, that becomes an ongoing Partnership. Each level leads naturally into the next, so you decide how far to go.

Three ways to work with us

Review & recommendations

Best for: a great place to start. You want a clear outside view before committing to anything.

A structured review of one part of your business and a short, written set of prioritised recommendations. No build included. Just a plan you can act on, with us or without us.

What's done
Half-day, on-site review of what you do and how you do it.
What's delivered
A written, prioritised action plan: yours to act on, with us or without us.
Duration
Half a day on site, plan in your inbox within 48 hours.

Fix one problem

Best for: you already know what's broken and want it solved.

An evaluation of that specific challenge, the right digital tool or service chosen and set up around how you work, and your team trained to run it day-to-day.

What's done
Evaluation of the problem, then the right tool chosen and configured around your existing workflow.
What's delivered
A working tool fitted to how you already work, and a team trained to run it themselves.
Duration
Typically 2–4 weeks from kickoff to your team owning it, with cost agreed upfront.

Ongoing partnership

Best for: the challenge is bigger than one fix, or keeps changing shape.

A full discovery of where digital and AI can genuinely help your business, delivery of the fix, and continuous support while it beds in, with your team trained to run it themselves, not kept dependent on us.

What's done
Full review across the business, not just one pain point, then delivery of each fix as it's agreed.
What's delivered
Problems solved as they come up, with your team trained to self-serve rather than dependent on us.
Duration
4 to 6 weeks or ongoing, with a check-in roughly every six months. No annual lock-in.
