How we work

Three ways to work with us, from a single audit to an ongoing partnership.

Small scope, real handover, no dependency on us afterwards — however far you decide to go.

An independent business doesn't have an IT department or an innovation team, but the AI and digital capability a large enterprise runs on doesn't need one — not anymore. Each of the three ways below hands you that capability without the sprawl of a dozen different platforms and logins. Pick the one that matches how much you want to hand over, then see the full breakdown of what we do and what we need from you.
