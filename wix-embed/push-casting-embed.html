The opportunity

An open casting, and you don't need experience

PUSH is a new comedy series for the BBC, produced by Various Artists Limited. We're casting from across West Yorkshire, and we want to hear from people who have never acted before as well as those who have.

This is a paid opportunity. You don't need training, an agent, a showreel, or a single credit to your name — just send us the few things listed below and we'll take it from there.
